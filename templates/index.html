📐 Mathematical Grounding: Why Ensemble Bagging Triumphs
In classical learning theory, an ensemble of M = 100 bagged trees each with variance σ² and mean pairwise correlation ρ yields total variance: Var(f̄) = ρσ² + ((1 - ρ) / M)σ². By injecting random feature subspace selection at every decision split (√p ≈ 4 features per node), correlation ρ is minimized. This drives variance down toward the theoretical lower bound without inflating bias—delivering a +1.74% to +10.34% performance gain over solitary estimators.
