💡 Why Random Forest Performs Best
Instead of relying on a single decision tree that can make mistakes or overfit, Random Forest builds 100 independent decision trees. Each tree analyzes a random subset of URL features and casts a vote. By averaging all 100 votes, individual errors cancel out, making the prediction much more reliable, stable, and accurate (achieving 85.88% accuracy and 89.94% recall on the debiased benchmark).
